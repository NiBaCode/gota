# Credits — Gota · Agua oscura (concept)

Gota is a fictional brand. Nothing on this page (house, bottle, batch, prices) is real.

## Hero (120 frames, desktop and phone)
- Rendered by us in Blender 3.6 Cycles. Scene: `sites/_src/nebbia-v2/acqua-3d/eclissi.py`.
- Bottle: 3D model «bluenight-20498», Magnific/Freepik (Nico's premium licence). Inner tube removed, materials rebuilt.
- Moon: NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio, CGI Moon Kit (https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720/), colour map and elevation map.
- Water: Blender Ocean modifier, no fluid simulation.

## Images
- `assets/img/nota-sal.webp`, `nota-iris.webp`, `nota-piedra.webp`, `ripples.webp`, `ripples-m.webp`: images generated with ChatGPT for the concept. Sources in `sites/_src/nebbia-v2/img-src/`.
- `assets/img/luna.webp`: NASA moon map rendered as a disc (`acqua-3d/luna.py`).
- `assets/img/fondo.webp`: still of the WebGL scene of section 4.

## Fonts (SIL Open Font License 1.1, licence texts in `assets/fonts/`)
- Bodoni Moda — The Bodoni Moda Project Authors (Indestructible Type). Latin subset from Google Fonts with Spanish accents, weights 400–500.
- Instrument Sans — The Instrument Sans Project Authors.

## Libraries
- GSAP 3.15 + ScrollTrigger — GreenSock, free standard licence (commercial use included).
- Lenis 1.3.27 — darkroom.engineering, MIT. `lenis.css` is inlined at the top of `styles.css`.

## Animation
- Animorio, «La goccia d'acqua che deforma il titolo sotto il puntatore» (`goccia-d-acqua-che-deforma-il-titolo`), https://animorio.com/animazioni/goccia-d-acqua-che-deforma-il-titolo/ — the drop lens (dome, refraction, rim, highlight, spring on the pointer) adapted in `assets/js/water.js` and `assets/js/main.js`.
